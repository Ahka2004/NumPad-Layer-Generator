  комбинации клавиш.(Например - Numpad5 and Numpad+ вы получаете "Layer 1a")
- Помощь - Numpad0 and ?
- Выход из программы - Numpad0 and /

We simplify work with automation and macros.

All you need to do is download the program file, 
press start, and your numpad (the numeric keypad on the right side of your keyboard) will turn 
into a powerful archaeological layer generator - Layer 1, Layer1a, Layer1b, Sterile layer....

A total of 40 standard combinations so you can enter them quickly and without looking, using just one hand.
---
Quick start! 
- Download the program and the cheat sheet for it.
- Run the program. You will see a hint in the dialog box. 
  Later, you can bring it back by pressing the key combination Numpad0 and ?
- Switch the numpad on your keyboard to number mode (NumLock key). The indicator on the keyboard will light up
- Example of use. Go to a table editor (e.g., Excel) or just a notepad, hover over any cell
  and press the Numpad1 key. The cell will display “Layer 1”.
  Using the cheat sheet (NumPad Layer Generator - CheatSheet), enter any other 
  key combinations. (For example, Numpad5 and Numpad+ will give you “Layer 1a”)
- Help - Numpad0 and ?
- Exit the program - Numpad0 and /

Enjoy!
